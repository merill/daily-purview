Month in brief

Purview will retire four DSPM partner integrations on December 7, 2026

The month’s clearest service-impacting deadline is the retirement of Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data sources. A broad set of updated Data Governance pages carries a separate transition message: Unified Catalog and classic Purview data governance capabilities are being retired and consolidated in Microsoft Fabric, and new accounts can no longer be created. The other major developments are forward-looking Data Lifecycle Management and Data Loss Prevention controls; much of the remaining volume repeats this governance guidance or clarifies existing behavior.

  • Purview · General
    DSPM partner integrations retire December 7

    Purview will retire the Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data sources on December 7, 2026. The integrations will no longer ingest or display data after retirement, while other DSPM capabilities remain unaffected.

  • Data Governance · Planning and deployment
    Updated governance guidance rules out new accounts

    Updated Microsoft Purview guidance now says Unified Catalog and classic Purview data governance capabilities are being retired and consolidated in Microsoft Fabric. New accounts can no longer be created.

  • Purview · General
    Priority cleanup adds approved hard deletion under retention

    Data Lifecycle Management’s priority cleanup workflow is scheduled for general availability in early December 2026 after a mid-August preview. It lets administrators hard-delete specified OneDrive and SharePoint content, override retention policies, and requires explicit setup with eDiscovery administrator approval.

  • Purview · General
    Azure Firewall integration extends DLP to network traffic

    Purview DLP’s Azure Firewall integration began preview rollout in mid-November 2026 and is scheduled for general availability by late April 2027. It covers sensitive network traffic, including text, files, and AI interactions; tenant activation is automatic, but administrators must configure policies. Existing policies are not disrupted.

  • Purview · Policies and rules
    SharePoint retention policies gain an archive path

    A November notice says Data Lifecycle Management will move inactive SharePoint files to Microsoft 365 Archive under retention policies starting in mid-January 2027. Archived files remain compliant and discoverable but are excluded from Copilot indexing, and administrator configuration is required. Another notice in the period gives September 2026 as the rollout start, creating a timing discrepancy.

For Purview administrators

Review dependencies on the four DSPM partner integrations before December 7; Microsoft says no action is required but recommends reviewing dependencies if they are in use. Do not plan new Unified Catalog or classic Purview data governance accounts based on the updated guidance; the supplied updates provide no migration deadline or procedure. Priority cleanup requires explicit setup and eDiscovery administrator approval, while Azure Firewall DLP requires policy configuration. The supplied SharePoint archive notices give different rollout timing, so verify the applicable schedule before planning deployment.
