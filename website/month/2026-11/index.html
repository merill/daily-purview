Purview sets December 7 retirement for four DSPM integrations as governance consolidates in Fabric

November was dominated by updated pages, but the material administrator signal is clear: four DSPM partner integrations for non-Microsoft data sources will stop ingesting and displaying data on December 7, 2026. Microsoft Learn also now carries broad guidance stating that Unified Catalog and classic Purview data governance capabilities are being retired and consolidated in Microsoft Fabric, with no new accounts available. Other significant changes cover DLP controls for external email in Copilot, lifecycle-aware adaptive scopes, and sensitivity labels for Microsoft Entra cloud security groups. Smaller updates clarify Insider Risk processing after group removal and rename failed sensitivity-label audit operations.

  • Purview · General
    Four DSPM partner integrations retire for non-Microsoft sources on December 7

    Microsoft Purview will retire the Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data sources on December 7, 2026. They will no longer ingest or display data, while other DSPM capabilities remain unaffected.

  • Data Governance · Troubleshooting
    Data Governance pages add no-new-account guidance as capabilities consolidate in Fabric

    A broad set of Microsoft Learn pages now states that Unified Catalog and classic Purview data governance capabilities are being retired and consolidated in Microsoft Fabric, and that new accounts can no longer be created. The supplied evidence gives no migration deadline or procedure.

  • Purview · AI and Copilot
    Copilot DLP will exclude external email from grounding

    The announced admin-configurable DLP control prevents Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Chat from using email from external or untrusted domains for grounding while retaining internal data use. The notice lists public preview in June 2026 and general availability in June 2027.

  • Purview · General
    Adaptive scopes will default to active recipients and site owners

    Adaptive scopes are being updated to evaluate only active recipients and site owners by default, with options to include inactive or soft-deleted objects. Queries will also support ISO 8601 durations; preview rollout starts in December 2026 and general availability in April 2027.

  • Purview · Labels and classification
    Sensitivity labels are coming to Microsoft Entra cloud security groups

    Starting in mid-January 2027, sensitivity labels can provide label-based governance and access policies for Microsoft Entra cloud security groups. The feature requires separate enablement and appropriate licensing, and applied labels cannot yet be changed or removed.

For Purview administrators

Review dependencies on Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust before December 7 if non-Microsoft data sources are involved; the notice says no action is required where these integrations are not used. For new governance work, treat the stated no-new-account and Fabric-consolidation guidance as a planning constraint; the supplied evidence gives no migration deadline or procedure. Track the announced Copilot DLP and adaptive-scope timelines. For Entra group labels, verify licensing and separate enablement, and account for labels not being changeable or removable after application.
