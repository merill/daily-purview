Purview retires four DSPM integrations as Copilot retention coverage splits in 2027

November's largest operational deadline is 7 December 2026, when Purview retires Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data; those integrations will stop ingesting and displaying data, while other DSPM capabilities remain. Copilot governance also gets two dated changes: a DLP control excludes external or untrusted-domain email from Copilot grounding, with general availability slated for June 2027, and the combined Teams/Copilot retention location becomes Teams-only on 15 January 2027. A separate Endpoint DLP notice points to mid-2027 AI Policy Sync and Device Health skills requiring Security Copilot. The remaining material is mainly operational clarification, including the tenant-wide auto-labeling ceiling and Cowork-related DLP guidance.

  • Purview · General
    Four non-Microsoft DSPM integrations retire on 7 December

    Purview will retire the Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data sources on 7 December 2026. They will stop ingesting and displaying data, while other DSPM capabilities remain unaffected. The notice says no action is required, but organizations using them should review dependencies.

  • Purview · AI and Copilot
    DLP control targets external email in Copilot grounding

    The admin-configurable control restricts Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Chat from processing email from external or untrusted domains by excluding that email from Copilot grounding while retaining internal data use. Message Center places public preview in June 2026 and general availability in June 2027.

  • Data Lifecycle · Policies and rules
    Retention location separates Teams content from Copilot interactions

    Beginning 15 January 2027, policies using “Teams chats and Copilot interactions” will apply only to Teams content. They will no longer retain or delete Microsoft 365 Copilot interactions, including previously retained interactions. Copilot coverage requires a separate policy using the “Microsoft Copilot Experiences” location.

  • Purview · General
    Edge for Business unmanaged-app DLP setup gains Intune approval workflow

    Starting in late November 2026, Purview DLP setup and activation for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business adds Intune multi-admin approval, clearer status, detailed errors, and task coordination between Purview and Intune administrators. No policy migration is needed.

  • Information Protection · Labels and classification
    Auto-labeling limit is an aggregate ceiling, not a daily rate

    The guidance defines 500,000 automatically labeled SharePoint and OneDrive files per tenant per day, aggregated across all auto-labeling policies, as an absolute maximum rather than a guaranteed processing rate. Actual throughput may be lower.

For Purview administrators

Review dependencies on the four DSPM connectors before 7 December if they are in use; the notice says no action is required otherwise. To preserve Copilot retention coverage, create a separate policy using the “Microsoft Copilot Experiences” location before the 15 January change. DLP administrators should account for the renamed Copilot/Cowork location and can prepare for the Edge/Intune approval workflow without migrating policies. Treat 500,000 files as an aggregate ceiling, not expected throughput; the later Endpoint DLP AI skills require Security Copilot.
