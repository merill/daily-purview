Day in brief

Unified Catalog guidance now records the no-new-account rule and November 12, 2027 timeline

This was largely a documentation-maintenance day. Updated guidance across Data Governance and related Purview pages consistently says Unified Catalog and classic data governance capabilities are being retired and consolidated in Microsoft Fabric: new data governance accounts cannot be created, while existing capabilities remain in maintenance mode through November 12, 2027. The guidance distinguishes unaffected data security and compliance capabilities. The main separate change is a DLP reference update for short pasted text.

  • Data Governance · Planning and deployment
    Retirement guidance standardizes the scope, account restriction, and Fabric destination

    The updated Unified Catalog plan states that Unified Catalog and classic data governance capabilities are being retired and consolidated in Microsoft Fabric. New governance accounts cannot be created, existing capabilities remain in maintenance mode through November 12, 2027, and security and compliance capabilities are unaffected.

  • Data Loss Prevention · Policies and rules
    DLP Paste to Browser coverage now has explicit short-text thresholds

    The DLP Policy Reference says Paste to Browser does not evaluate text smaller than 15 characters or 30 bytes. Administrators should use those thresholds when assessing DLP coverage for short pasted text.
