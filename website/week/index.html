Week in brief

Purview Adaptive protection preview adds 120-day retention for elevated-risk deletions

Data Lifecycle Management’s Adaptive protection integration is the week’s clearest capability change: the public preview applies retention labels to content deleted by elevated-risk users for 120 days, with general availability listed for early 2027. Other notices set out a future DLP workflow improvement for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business and an Azure PST Import path, while documentation adds operational guidance for Clipchamp labeling and Teams Information Barriers.

  • Purview · General
    Adaptive protection adds a 120-day deletion safeguard in public preview

    Data Lifecycle Management’s public-preview integration automatically applies retention labels to content deleted by elevated-risk users for 120 days. The message lists general availability for early 2027 and says the setup is minimal.

  • Purview · General
    Edge for Business DLP gains clearer multi-admin approval coordination

    The planned unmanaged-app workflow adds clearer status, error reporting, and task coordination for Intune multi-admin approval. Rollout starts January 2027; no prior action is required, existing policies remain unchanged, and Intune approvals continue as before.

  • Purview · General
    Azure PST Import outlines PowerShell ingestion from Blob Storage to Exchange Online

    The notice describes importing PST files from Azure Blob Storage into Exchange Online mailboxes with PowerShell, readiness analysis, and import batching. It requires specific Azure permissions and mailbox prerequisites, while imported data remains subject to existing retention and audit policies.

  • Information Protection · Labels and classification
    Clipchamp guidance maps one tenant setting to project and MP4 labels

    The updated Information Protection guidance says `EnableSensitivityLabelForVideoFiles` controls Clipchamp project labeling and whether exported MP4 files inherit the project’s label. Administrators should use the tenant’s current value when assessing those outcomes.

  • Communication Compliance · General
    Teams Information Barriers guidance adds explicit-mode conversion timing and limits

    The updated page gives PowerShell instructions to change a Teams-connected Microsoft 365 group from Implicit to Explicit mode and says segment stamping can take up to 24 hours. It also documents segment-based access and membership, unsupported transitions from Owner moderated or Open mode, and the inability of site owners or SharePoint admins to manage segments on Explicit-mode Teams-connected sites.

For Purview administrators

Administrators evaluating the preview should account for its 120-day retention-label behavior. The Edge DLP update requires no prior action and leaves existing policies unchanged. Plan Explicit-mode Teams group changes around the documented 24-hour segment-stamping window, check the tenant value of `EnableSensitivityLabelForVideoFiles` for Clipchamp scenarios, and note that PST imports require specific Azure permissions and mailbox prerequisites.
