Week in brief

Purview sets a December deadline for four DSPM partner integrations

Purview will retire Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data sources on 7 December 2026. Separately, Data Loss Prevention is improving unmanaged-app setup in Edge for Business with Intune multi-admin approval, while permissions auditing gains two new operations. Updated guidance also defines Copilot and Cowork label-inheritance limits and explains Insider Risk profile associations.

  • Purview · General
    Four DSPM partner integrations stop ingesting and displaying data on 7 December

    Microsoft will retire the Varonis, BigID, Cyera, and OneTrust integrations for non-Microsoft data sources on 7 December 2026. The integrations will no longer ingest or display data; other DSPM capabilities remain unaffected. Microsoft says no action is required, but administrators should review dependencies on these integrations.

  • Purview · General
    Edge for Business unmanaged-app DLP setup adds Intune approval coordination

    Starting in late November 2026, Purview DLP setup and activation for unmanaged apps in Edge for Business will provide clearer status information, detailed errors, and task coordination between Purview and Intune administrators. Intune multi-admin approval is added, and existing DLP policies do not need to be migrated.

  • Purview · General
    Purview permissions auditing gains two new RBAC operations

    The permissions audit log is being enhanced with two additional audit operations to improve visibility into role-based access control, troubleshooting, and access investigations. The worldwide rollout runs from late September through mid-November 2026 and requires no administrator action.

  • DSPM · AI and Copilot
    DSPM guidance defines Cowork content-label inheritance and its limits

    Updated guidance states that new Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and PDF content can inherit the source item's sensitivity label and protection settings. User-defined-permission labels are unsupported, and labeling does not occur when the label is unavailable to the user or the file exceeds 50 MB.

  • Insider Risk · General
    Insider Risk guidance explains alternate-email profile associations

    The updated Insider Risk page says activity and historical activity can be associated with user profiles through alternate email addresses, including Microsoft Entra ID's Other mail values. Changing an address does not correct existing alerts or historical associations, so administrators should review alternate addresses when activity appears under an unexpected profile.

For Purview administrators

Review dependencies on the four retiring DSPM integrations before 7 December; those integrations will stop ingesting and displaying data, although other DSPM capabilities remain unaffected. For Edge for Business unmanaged-app DLP, coordinate Purview and Intune administrators as the updated setup rolls out; no policy migration is required. Use the updated DSPM and Insider Risk guidance when assessing labeling coverage or investigating activity associated with an unexpected user profile.
