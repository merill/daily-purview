Week in brief

Copilot DLP will exclude external email from grounding as retention coverage splits in 2027

Purview’s most consequential update is an admin-configurable DLP control that excludes email from external or untrusted domains from Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Chat grounding while preserving internal data use. The Message Center notice lists public preview in June 2026 and general availability in June 2027. Retention guidance separately sets January 15, 2027, as the point when “Teams chats and Copilot interactions” covers Teams content only; the remaining updates are mainly operational guidance for auto-labeling, simulations, and audit review.

  • Purview · AI and Copilot
    DLP adds external-email controls for Copilot grounding

    Microsoft Purview is expanding DLP with an admin-configurable control for Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Chat. It restricts processing of emails from external or untrusted domains, excluding them from Copilot grounding while internal data use continues. The Message Center notice lists public preview in June 2026 and general availability in June 2027.

  • Data Lifecycle · Policies and rules
    Teams retention location drops Copilot interactions in 2027

    Beginning January 15, 2027, policies using “Teams chats and Copilot interactions” will apply only to Teams content and will no longer retain or delete Microsoft 365 Copilot interactions, including previously retained interactions. The guidance directs administrators to use a separate “Microsoft Copilot Experiences” location when Copilot retention is required.

  • Information Protection · Labels and classification
    The 500,000-file auto-labeling ceiling applies across all policies

    Information Protection guidance now defines 500,000 automatically labeled SharePoint and OneDrive files per tenant per day, aggregated across all auto-labeling policies, as an absolute ceiling. It is not a guaranteed processing rate, and actual throughput may be lower.

  • Information Protection · Labels and classification
    Simulation results distinguish failed jobs from requests that never started

    The Auto Label Simulation Results guidance adds the “Simulation failed” status, distinguishes failed jobs from requests that could not start, explains partial workload counts, and provides review and restart guidance. Administrators can preserve useful results before restarting and inspect workload details before enabling a policy.

  • Audit · General
    Audit references add Power BI MCP semantic-model activities

    The Audit Log Activities reference now lists events for Power BI MCP tools that modify semantic models and read semantic-model metadata. Administrators can use these activity names when reviewing or interpreting audit records.

For Purview administrators

If Copilot retention coverage is required from January 15, 2027, create a separate retention policy using the “Microsoft Copilot Experiences” location; Teams retention behavior is unchanged. Plan auto-labeling against the 500,000-file-per-tenant-per-day aggregate ceiling across all policies, rather than treating it as a guaranteed rate. Use the added simulation statuses and audit activity names when interpreting results and records.
