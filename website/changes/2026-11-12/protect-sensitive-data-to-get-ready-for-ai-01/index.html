Protect Sensitive Data To Get Ready For Ai

12 November 2026Doc update+14 / −8 lines

In brief

The article now uses action-oriented wording for its recommendations and adds a Summary section explaining how Purview can help admins understand data, protect sensitive information, investigate risks, and govern AI interactions.

What Purview admins need to know

Admins get clearer guidance for identifying and addressing data-protection gaps before AI apps and agents use organizational data.

Organizations can accelerate AI adoption by treating data readiness as a continuous operating model rather than a one-time deployment checkpoint. This approach helps ensure that the right people, apps, and agents can access the right data with the right permissions and protections as data, access, and AI usage evolve. Extending the same trusted foundation across supported human activity, AI apps, agents, and models enables organizations to:

  • UnderstandingUnderstand where your sensitive data lives.
  • GainingGain visibility into what data AI can access across apps, browsers, on-premises, file shares and other endpoints.
  • ScalingScale classification, policy creation, investigations, and compliance reporting to AI usage.
  • EnsuringEnsure the right permissions and controls are in place to help prevent oversharing.
  • MaintainingMaintain consistent protections as data grows and spreads across systems.
  • Proactively identifyingidentify and remediatingremediate issues before they become data security and/or compliance incidents.
  • ProvidingProvide measurable proof of data protection to regulators, auditors, executives, and/or customers.

Solution

  • Help increase security ROI by using classification and labeling to scale consistent protection, help reduce manual effort, and help prevent sensitive data from being leaked or overshared.
  • Scale AI adoption with confidence by establishing protection for sensitive information as a foundation and continuously addressing data exposure as usage expands.
  • Maintain consistent protection at scale so teams can collaborate, adopt AI, an expand data use while policies continue to follow sensitive information across supported systems. \ No newline at end of file
  • Increase executive confidence with demonstratable policy effectiveness and audit readiness across your organization.

Summary

The recommended actions can help you secure your data, which is foundational to confidently adopting AI. Microsoft Purview can help you understand your data, protect what is sensitive, investigate data risks, and govern AI interactions.

By prioritizing data readiness, you can help identify and address protection gaps before AI apps and agents put your data to work. \ No newline at end of file \ No newline at end of file
