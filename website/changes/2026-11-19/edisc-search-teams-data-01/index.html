Edisc Search Teams Data

19 November 2026Doc update+14 / −16 lines

In brief

The page clarifies that cmdlet purges affect only mailbox compliance copies, while Graph purges targeting `teamsMessages` permanently delete Teams messages regardless of `purgeType`. Separate requests are required to purge both copies.

What Purview admins need to know

Use the documented purge-area distinctions to avoid leaving user-visible messages in Teams or permanently deleting messages unintentionally.
