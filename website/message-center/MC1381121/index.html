Microsoft Purview | Endpoint Data Loss Prevention: Policy sync and device health AI skill

10 November 2026MC1381121 on mc.merill.net ↗Message

In brief

Microsoft Purview Endpoint Data Loss Prevention introduces AI-powered Policy Sync and Device Health skills for real-time visibility into policy deployment and sync issues. Rolling out mid-2027, it helps admins identify impacted devices, root causes, and remediation actions, requiring Security Copilot enabled in the tenant.

Message Center announcement

Updated November 10, 2026: We have updated the timeline. Thank you for your patience.  

What and Why

We are introducing Policy Sync and Device Health AI skills for Microsoft Purview Endpoint Data Loss Prevention. These AI-powered capabilities provide real-time visibility into policy deployment across devices and help admins quickly identify and resolve sync issues. By using AI to surface root causes and recommend actions, this feature improves troubleshooting efficiency and increases confidence that policies are enforced consistently across devices. This aligns with our enterprise-ready AI approach by delivering intelligent insights within Microsoft Purview.

This message is associated with Microsoft 365 Roadmap ID 562016.

Rollout Schedule

  • Public Preview: We will begin rolling out early June 2027 and expect to complete by early June 2027. 
  • Worldwide: We will begin rolling out early July 2027 and expect to complete by rolling out early July 2027

Impact on Your Organization

Who is affected

  • Admins managing Endpoint Data Loss Prevention policies in Microsoft Purview

Platforms/Services

  • Microsoft Purview compliance portal
  • Endpoint DLP
  • Security Copilot

What will happen

  • Admins will see new AI-powered insights for policy sync status and device health: 
  • Real-time visibility into DLP policy deployment across endpoints will be available: 
  • Impacted devices and sync failures will be identified automatically: 
  • Root cause explanations and recommended remediation actions will be provided: 
  • The experience will be available within DLP policy and device views in Microsoft Purview.
  • The feature requires Security Copilot capabilities to be available in the tenant.

Action Required/Recommendations

No immediate action is required.

To prepare for this feature:

  • Ensure that Security Copilot is enabled in your organization.
  • Confirm that Security Compute Units are provisioned to support AI experiences.
  • Verify that admins have appropriate permissions to access DLP policies and device views in Microsoft Purview.
  • Review the DLP portal to familiarize your team with where the feature will appear.
  • Communicate this update to your security and compliance administrators.

Compliance considerations

The change introduces AI-powered skills that analyze DLP policy sync status and device health, identify impacted devices, and provide root cause insights along with recommended remediation actions.
